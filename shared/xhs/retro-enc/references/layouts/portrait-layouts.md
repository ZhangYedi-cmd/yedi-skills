## Part 1: Cover Archetypes 封面版型库（6种）

> 核心原则：**每组系列的封面必须根据内容特点选择最匹配的版型，禁止全部默认 center-radial**。

### cover-A 物品环绕型（Central Object + Organic Scatter）
```
Large central hero object (steamer, bowl, vase, bookshelf, etc.) occupying 30-40%
of center area on aged Chinese paper. Surrounding items scattered in NATURAL,
ASYMMETRIC arrangement (NOT a rigid symmetric ring). Items vary in size — some
overlap, some tilt, some partially hidden behind others. Each item with Chinese
name label. Bold Chinese title「[标题]」at top, English subtitle below.
Red square stamps at corners. Credit at bottom.
```
适用：品种合集（饺子馅、茶叶、花卉）；有明确"容器"概念的主题
参考：博主「12种超好吃的饺子馅合集」— 蒸笼居中，食材自然散落四周

### cover-B 多人群像型（Character Ensemble）
```
[N] distinct historical Chinese figures arranged in ORGANIC GROUPING (not a
perfect ring) on aged Chinese paper. Each figure in different pose and outfit
representing their role/profession. Figures vary in size — front 2-3 larger,
back ones smaller. Each with Chinese name label and thin annotation line.
Very large bold title at top. Red square stamps at corners.
Ink-wash landscape elements in background corners.
```
适用：角色/人物分类（八大天规、职业类、人物传记类）
参考：博主「中国人的"八大天规"」— 8位古代人物错落排列

### cover-C 标本解剖型（Botanical/Specimen Plate）
```
Single large main specimen illustration (plant, animal, object) taking up 50-60%
of center area on aged Chinese paper. Highly detailed with fine-line ink contour
and rich saturated watercolor fills. Multiple annotation lines to Chinese labels
pointing to different parts. Small scene vignettes (2-3) around edges showing
usage/care/habitat. Title at top-right. Red stamps at corners.
Bottom row: variety comparison strip showing related sub-types.
```
适用：单品深度解析（单个植物、单个动物、单个器物）；品种鉴赏
参考：博主「君子兰」— 大株植物居中，四周标注和养护场景

### cover-D 流程变换型（Transformation / Step Flow）
```
Left side (35%): BEFORE state illustration with label.
Right side (35%): AFTER state illustration with label.
Between them: transformation arrow or numbered step flow (Step 01 → Step 10)
going down the center. Each step with small product/tool illustration.
Large bold title at top. Red stamps at corners.
Decorative elements (flowers, leaves, sparkles) scattered along transformation path.
```
适用：变化过程（化妆步骤、翻新、改造、成长）；前后对比类
参考：博主「10步从素颜到精致」— 左素颜右精致，中间步骤流

### cover-E 分区分类型（Zoned Category Display）
```
Page divided into [N] named zones/sections, each zone containing 3-5 items.
Zones separated by subtle dividers or background color shifts.
Zone labels in bold Chinese with descriptive subtitle.
Items within each zone illustrated as a mini-cluster. Central connecting element
(logo, seal, or bridge illustration) linking all zones.
Large bold title at top. Red stamps at corners.
Bottom: comparison table or budget reference chart.
```
适用：按维度分类的多选指南（选狗、选车、选房、选专业）
参考：博主「人生第一只狗怎么选」— 小白区/学生区/家庭区分区

### cover-F 场景多格型（Multi-Scene Grid / Compilation）
```
Page divided into [N] scene panels (4-12 panels), each panel showing a different
scenario/scene with its own Chinese title label and small illustration.
Panels in irregular grid — NOT perfectly uniform sizes.
Some panels slightly larger (hero scenes). Thin borders between panels.
Overall title at the very top spanning full width.
Red stamps at corners. Aged paper texture throughout.
```
适用：场景集锦（四大系列、民俗场景、生活百态）；系列合集预览
参考：博主「四大系列合集」— 12个"四大X"格子场景

### ⚡ Cover Archetype Auto-Selection

| 内容信号 | 推荐 Cover | 理由 |
|----------|-----------|------|
| N种品种/种类 + 有"容器"概念 | cover-A 物品环绕 | 容器聚焦，品种散落 |
| N个人物/角色/职业 | cover-B 多人群像 | 人物群像叙事 |
| 单一主题深度解析 | cover-C 标本解剖 | 单品放大+标注 |
| 步骤/流程/变化/对比 | cover-D 流程变换 | 前后转变可视化 |
| 按维度/人群分类的选择指南 | cover-E 分区分类 | 分区对比易读 |
| 多场景/多主题合集预览 | cover-F 场景多格 | 多格展示丰富度 |
| 通用/不确定 | cover-A 或 cover-B | 最安全的两种 |

## Part 2: Content Page Layouts 内容页版型

### annotated 解剖标注（知识图解叠层）
```
Upper half: [subject] illustration on aged Chinese paper with [N] annotation
lines to Chinese labels. Red square stamps top-left "[系列标签]" and top-right
"[主题词]". Very large bold Chinese main title below illustration.
Lower half: structured knowledge panels — [M] boxed sections with ① ② ③ ④
numbered items, each with brief Chinese description.
[If applicable] ✓ correct example scene on right, ✗ incorrect example on left.
Bottom: tool/item reference diagram OR summary quote box in italic style.
Credit: 作者：@[署名] at bottom.
```
插画占40-55%，知识块占35-45%，标注4-10条

### knowledge-split 知识分栏（博主内容页主力版型）
```
Top (15%): Very large bold Chinese title "[核心词]". Red square stamps at corners.
Upper middle (40%): Central illustration — [subject] with annotation lines.
Below illustration: thin horizontal divider.
Lower section split into 2-3 columns of knowledge boxes:
  Left column: [知识块1标题] with ① ② ③ items
  Right column: [知识块2标题] with bullet items or tool diagram
Bottom row: [智慧点评框 / 总结金句] in bordered italic box.
Credit: 作者：@[署名].
```
适合 anatomy/process 变体的内容页

### single-subject-plate 单品专页（一页一主题 ⭐ 推荐默认）
```
Top (10%): Very large bold title "[单品名称]" + subtitle "[分类说明]".
Red corner stamps: left "[系列标签]" right "[特质词]".
Upper area (45%): Large hero illustration of THIS SINGLE [subject], centered,
highly detailed with annotation lines to key features/components.
Left sidebar: ingredient/component list with small illustrations.
Right sidebar: step-by-step process OR trait list with numbered steps.
Bottom (25%): 2-3 info cards in a row: [配比/数据] [特点描述] [使用建议].
Very bottom: summary box + credit.
```
> ⭐ **列表类内容的首选版型**：当主题是"N种X"时（龙生九子、12种饺子馅、
> 八大天规），每个X独占一页，此版型让单一主体获得充分展示空间。
>
> 参考：博主「玉米木耳猪肉馅」「韭菜鸡蛋馅」— 每种馅独占一页

### grid-surround 九宫环绕
```
Central [subject] in the middle on aged Chinese paper, surrounded by [N]
companion items in symmetrical grid. Each with Chinese name label.
Red square stamps at top corners. Faint traditional Chinese landscape or
ink-wash mountain background. Chinese title at top.
```
主体30-40%，配件8-12个，适合配对/目录类

### fusion-plate 融合图版
```
Top: silhouette comparison of [A] and [B] with plus/equals signs leading to [C].
Center (40%): large [C] illustration with annotation lines in Chinese.
Sides: behavioral vignettes. Bottom: comparison table and trait list.
Red square corner stamps. Chinese title at top.
```

### sequential 动态序列
```
Central [subject] in action with numbered (①②③) trajectory paths or step
sequence. Scene vignettes showing stages. Step description boxes alongside.
Red square corner stamp top-left for series label. Large Chinese title.
```
轨迹/步骤节点4-8个，适合 process 变体
