## organic-poster 有机海报（唯一主版面）

```
Portrait (3:4). Subject(s) expand freely in organic composition, NOT confined
to rigid panels. Illustration breathes naturally — subjects can cross zones,
overlap each other, extend to canvas edges.

Title zone (top 15%): brush calligraphy main title at top, subtitle below,
English line below subtitle. Red square stamps top-left and top-right.

Illustration zone (mid 75%): main subject(s) placed organically — portrait
figure can dominate one full side vertically. Supporting elements scattered
naturally around primary subject. Knowledge annotation lines extend from
subjects. Scattered red seal stamps and background depth elements within this zone.

Bottom zone (10%): dot-separated attribute list OR wisdom quote in red-bordered box.
Credit: 作者：@知渡 at very bottom.
```

**适用**：所有主题的内容页；封面按构图选择版面子模式

**知识区块位置（内容页）**：
- 分栏知识块：左右各一列，在插画两侧或插画下方
- 单列知识框：插画下方 35-45% 区域
- 底部总结框：朱砂红边框，楷体斜体

## dense-cluster 密集簇拥（group-portrait 封面专用）v3.3

> group-portrait 封面使用此模式。center-radial 封面使用 focal-radial 模式。

```
DENSELY PACKED composition filling the entire canvas edge to edge.
Minimal white space. Elements tightly clustered, overlapping at edges —
creating tapestry-like richness, NOT isolated specimens on blank paper.

[OPTIONAL] OUTER FRAME: Thin rectangular border line around the composition,
slightly inset from canvas edges. Use for cultural/zodiac/formal themes.
[If used]: Chinese-style corner ornaments (dragon motifs 龙纹 / cloud scrolls
云纹) at four corners of the border frame.

Anchor figure: ONE dominant subject occupying the ENTIRE LEFT SIDE (or RIGHT SIDE)
vertically — this is the LARGEST element, significantly bigger than others.

Supporting cast: 6-10 secondary subjects clustered TIGHTLY around the anchor,
overlapping each other and the anchor figure. Loose scattered elements (stones,
drops, petals, leaves) fill any remaining gaps.

Background depth: faint architectural or landscape silhouettes in atmospheric
ink wash behind all subjects — VERY FAINT, purely atmospheric.

[OPTIONAL] SUBJECT NAME TAGS: For character/creature collections (zodiac, figures),
each subject can carry a small banner/scroll/flag with their name attached to
their body — creates "museum specimen label" effect.

STAMPS: Red square stamps at 2-4 corners (top-left + top-right required,
bottom-left + bottom-right optional for cultural/formal themes).
Additional 2-3 scattered red seal stamps throughout.

NO empty zones. NO isolated floating subjects. NO grid-like regular spacing.
Fill every quadrant with subjects, decorations, or background depth elements.
```

**v3.3 新增**：外框线+角花对 dense-cluster 变为可选（文化/生肖/正式主题启用）。

## focal-radial 中心放射（center-radial 封面专用）v3.3 ★ 重大更新

> center-radial 封面强制使用此模式。规则/方法/哲理/戒律/处世类主题。
> **v3.3 核心变更**：背景框 S/M/L 三档弹性尺寸；深色背景框支持；元素可骑框；四角印章+角花；侧文区。

```
OUTER FRAME: Thin rectangular decorative border line around the entire composition,
inset approximately 1-2% from canvas edges. Sepia brown (#5C3D2E) line.
[Optional] Chinese-style corner ornaments (cloud scrolls 云纹 / dragon motifs 龙纹
/ ruyi patterns 如意纹) at four corners of the border frame.
Creates a "framed encyclopedia page" feel. NOT Victorian, NOT ornate Western.

Title zone (top 12-15%): brush calligraphy main title ABOVE the center illustration,
subtitle and English line below title.

CENTER BACKDROP (mid-canvas center):
A [circular vignette / rectangular frame] — choose SHAPE based on theme:
  - Circular: philosophy / Taoism / nature themes
  - Rectangular: historical figures / rules / precepts themes
Choose SIZE based on element count + subject type:
  - S (25-35% canvas): ≤6 elements, small subject (lotus/symbol/vessel)
  - M (35-50% canvas): 7-9 elements OR seated figure (sage/historical figure)
  - L (50-65% canvas): ≥10 elements OR large scene — elements may straddle frame
Choose TONE based on mood:
  - Light ink-wash (default): mountain/bamboo/palace scene, medium visibility
  - Dark indigo (#1B2838): starry sky/galaxy/lighthouse — dramatic contrast
  - Dark forest (#1A3C2A): dense misty forest — mysterious atmosphere
Thin decorative border on the backdrop frame.

CENTER SUBJECT: [main figure/object] placed IN FRONT of (overlapping) the backdrop
frame. Has soft warm golden radiant glow (#F5C842) emanating from it.

RADIAL ARRANGEMENT (surrounding the center):
[N] symbolic objects/elements at even clock positions (12, 2, 4, 6, 8, 10 o'clock).
[If L-size backdrop]: some elements STRADDLE the backdrop frame boundary —
partially inside, partially outside — breaking the rigid circle/frame edge.
[If S/M-size]: elements outside the frame with clear breathing room.
Each is a complete detailed illustration with:
  - Chinese label name (annotation line)
  - Circled number ①②③④⑤⑥...
  - Brief description (2-6 characters)
ORGANIZED radial wheel, not random scatter.

STAMPS: Red square stamps at 2-4 corners:
  - Required: top-left + top-right
  - Optional: bottom-left + bottom-right (for cultural/图鉴 themes)
  - Additional 2-3 scattered red seal stamps throughout

[OPTIONAL] SIDE VERTICAL TEXT: wisdom quotes or couplets in vertical brush
calligraphy along left and/or right margins, reading top to bottom.
E.g., left: "道可道，非常道", right: "上善若水，厚德载物".

Bottom zone (10%): Choose one:
  - Dot-separated attribute list (default): "attr1 · attr2 · attr3"
  - Ribbon banner: decorative scroll/ribbon strip with summary text
  - Publisher bar: faux-publication line with series name + catalog number
Credit: 作者：@知渡.
Loose scattered elements (coins, petals, ink drops) fill remaining minor gaps.
```

**封面 Prompt 必加语言（focal-radial v3.3）**：
```
Thin rectangular decorative border frame around entire composition (inset 1-2%).
[Optional: Chinese corner ornaments (云纹/龙纹) at four frame corners.]

containing atmospheric Chinese [landscape] scene.
Main subject [描述] overlapping the backdrop with warm golden glow.
Surrounding: [N] objects at regular clock positions in organized radial wheel.
[If L-size: some elements straddle the frame boundary.]
Each labeled with Chinese name + description, circled number, annotation line.
[Optional: vertical wisdom quotes along left/right margins.]
Stamps at [2/4] corners + [2-3] scattered seals.
Bottom: [dot-list / ribbon banner / publisher bar].
```

## annotated 解剖标注（内容页子模式）

```
Upper half: subject illustration with [N] thin annotation lines to Chinese labels.
Red square stamps at corners. Large brush calligraphy title at top.
Lower half: structured knowledge panels — [M] boxed sections with ① ② ③ ④
numbered items. [If applicable] ✓ correct scene (right) vs ✗ incorrect scene (left).
Bottom: summary quote box in bordered italic style.
Credit: 作者：@知渡.
```

插画占40-55%，知识块占35-45%，标注4-10条
